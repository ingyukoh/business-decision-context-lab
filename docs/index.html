What the semantic layer supplies
sales —unit→ thousands of units
sales —predicted_by→ OLS —trained_on→ Advertising
sales / Advertising —source→ statlearning.com/s/Advertising.csv
OLS —limitation→ association only; no causal ROI or optimal budget
Advertising —structure→ cross-sectional, not time-series
SQLite recursive traversal supplies these definitions. It is a small hand-defined semantic layer, not an enterprise ontology or Databricks implementation. Every output requires human review; no external action is executed.
