3. Evaluate the evidence, including failures

RMSE is in thousands of sales units. Reproducible 120/40/40 split of 200 public observations. The 0.5B causal language model is not fine-tuned here and is not a frontier-model substitute.
The initial four recorded generations produced unit errors, including “million units” instead of “thousands of units.” Narrow number/unit/caveat checks passed 1 of 2 outputs with context and 0 of 2 without. Two examples per condition do not establish a general improvement. The validator was revised retrospectively to accept synonymous units; raw generations were retained.


What this addresses for a client
  • Keep numeric model output separate from narrative generation.
  • Resolve units, provenance and limitations before explaining a metric.
  • Reject malformed inputs and retain failed explanations for review.
  • Expose inspectable deployment, source, model and evaluation artifacts.
For a pet/garden business, the next step is to validate an agreed metric on its authorized sales data, compare with its current baseline, and map those definitions into its platform. This demo does not establish prior Databricks delivery or production Claude/GPT operation.
